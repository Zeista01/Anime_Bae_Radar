# Anime Bae Radar: Find Your Anime Crush!

Welcome to **Anime Bae Radar**—your one-stop destination to discover that perfect anime crush who matches your personality! Whether you're a seasoned otaku or new to the anime world, our platform is designed to ensure that every match speaks to who you are.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Take the Quiz
Begin your journey by taking our **Personalized Matching Quiz**. Answer carefully curated questions about:
- Your likes and dislikes
- Hobbies and interests
- Values and personality traits

This fun and engaging quiz helps us build a profile that captures the essence of your unique character.

### 2. Advanced Matching Algorithm
After submitting your quiz:
- Our **advanced algorithm** analyzes your responses using a mix of psychological profiling and machine learning techniques.
- Your profile is compared with an extensive database of anime characters, each tagged with detailed traits and story arcs.
- Compatibility scores are calculated, ensuring that the recommended characters truly reflect your personality.

### 3. Discover Your Anime Crush
Once the matching process is complete, you’ll receive a personalized list of anime characters. These aren’t just random suggestions—they are carefully curated matches with the highest compatibility scores just for you!

## Share Your Results
